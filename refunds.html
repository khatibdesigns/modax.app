Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Khatib Designs Ltd

Terms of ServicePrivacy PolicyRefund Policy

Simple version: if you are not happy, ask within 14 days of a payment and you get all of it back.

1. 14-day refund on every payment

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.

You can get a full refund within 14 days of any MODAX payment — your first Growth payment, a monthly renewal, added seats or a marketplace add-on — for any reason.

  • How: request it at paddle.net using the email from your receipt, or email hello@modax.app and we will process it with Paddle.
  • Where the money goes: back to the original payment method, including tax. Banks usually show it within 5–10 business days.
  • What happens to the workspace: when a refund covers the current period, the workspace moves to the free Starter plan. Your data stays.

2. After 14 days

Payments older than 14 days are generally not refunded, but we review every request fairly — for example a duplicate charge, a charge after you cancelled, or a serious fault we could not fix. Email hello@modax.app.

3. Cancelling

Cancel any time from your workspace billing settings, from the link in your Paddle receipt or at paddle.net, or by emailing hello@modax.app. You keep Growth features until the end of the month you have paid for, then the workspace moves to Starter and is not charged again. To have the current month refunded, ask within 14 days of that payment.

4. Free and Scale plans

The Starter plan is free, so there is nothing to refund. Scale plans follow their order form; if paid through Paddle, the 14-day refund above applies to each payment.

5. Your legal rights

This policy does not reduce any rights you have under the consumer or commercial law of your country.
